What Catty is

Catty gives friendly AI visual estimates of a cat’s likely breed from a photo. Results are estimates for fun and learning — cats are wonderfully individual.

Not a vet

Catty is not a veterinary, medical, or DNA service and doesn’t diagnose health. For anything health-related, please see a licensed vet.
